Boss encounters

Bosses

Kraab hunts the watch, Vilgax answers a beacon, and Zs'Skayr waits in the Null Void.

Kraab

Kraab closes in for melee attacks and fires bolts at range.

80 health · 12 attack damage
Drop: Kraab Claw (1 to 2)
Find or summon: The first watch bind schedules Kraab 2 to 5 game minutes later, at a valid spot 16 to 32 blocks away.

Vilgax

Vilgax uses melee attacks and a ground slam. At half health he enters phase two, gains speed and resistance, and calls in drones.

400 health · 18 attack damage
Drop: Vilgax Core
Find or summon: Craft the Vilgax Beacon in the Omnitrix Crafter. Use it on top of a solid block in the Overworld.

Vilgax Drone

Drones fly after the player, dash toward nearby targets, and attack at close range.

16 health · 3 attack damage
Drop: No drops listed in the entity loot table.
Find or summon: Vilgax calls drones during phase two.

Zs'Skayr

Zs'Skayr fires shadow bolts, summons Null Void Prisoners, and turns invisible briefly as he blinks.

500 health · 12 attack damage
Drop: Master Control
Find or summon: Search the Null Void fortress. The fortress feature has a 1-in-400-chunks rarity filter.

Vilgax Beacon

The Beacon recipe uses a Kraab Claw, Black Diamond, Infinitum and a Redstone Block. View the recipe. The summon starts after a short beacon animation. Vilgax's loot table lists a Vilgax Core, used with Infinitum to craft the Null Void Projector.

Vilgax Drones have 16 health and 3 attack damage. The Vilgax entity removes its own drones when it dies.
