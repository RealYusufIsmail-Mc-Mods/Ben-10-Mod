Put on the Omnitrix and turn into aliens from the original Ben 10 series. Unlock new aliens by scanning DNA, fight
Kraab, Vilgax and Zs'Skayr, and travel to the Null Void. Minecraft 1.20.1, for Forge (also NeoForge) and Fabric.

## Getting started

1. Find the Omnitrix in a rare crash-site crater, or build it in the Omnitrix Crafter.
2. Right-click to strap it on. It stays on your wrist for good.
3. Press `G` (or right-click the air with an empty hand) to open the dial, pick an alien and transform.
4. Press `V` to use your alien's power, and `H` to change back.

Every new player gets the **Omnitrix Field Guide**, a book that explains the rest.

## The aliens

![The 10 aliens](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RealYusufIsmail-Mc-Mods/Ben-10-Mod/1.20.1/docs/images/aliens.png)

You start with Heatblast, Four Arms and XLR8. Unlock the others by sneaking and right-clicking creatures and blocks
with an empty hand to scan their DNA. Each alien has its own size, abilities and power, for example Heatblast's
fireballs, Four Arms' ground slam and XLR8's dash. A transformation lasts 5 minutes; then the Omnitrix needs a minute
to recharge.

## Bosses and the Null Void

![Bosses and the Null Void](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RealYusufIsmail-Mc-Mods/Ben-10-Mod/1.20.1/docs/images/bosses.png)

- **Kraab** hunts you down a few minutes after you first put on the Omnitrix.
- **Vilgax** arrives when you use a Vilgax Beacon. Below half health he sends in drones.
- **The Null Void** is a dimension of floating islands, reached with the Null Void Projector. Escaped prisoners roam
  it, and rare fortresses hold **Zs'Skayr**.
- **Master Control**, dropped by Zs'Skayr, unlocks every alien and removes the time limit.

## Omnitrix Crafter

![Omnitrix Crafter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RealYusufIsmail-Mc-Mods/Ben-10-Mod/1.20.1/docs/images/crafter.png)

The Crafter makes the Omnitrix parts, Ben 10 gear, the Vilgax Beacon and the Null Void Projector.
