Roadmap

What's out, what's being built now, and what comes next.

Released versions are on CurseForge. In progress is being built and tested now. Planned releases may change.
2.0.0
Released 14 Oct 2026

The Omnitrix

Minecraft 1.20.1 on Forge and Fabric. The Omnitrix with 10 aliens in 3D, DNA scanning, alien powers on V, H to change back, a visible time limit and recharge, the Omnitrix Field Guide, the Crafter recipe chain, Kraab, Vilgax, the Null Void, Zs'Skayr and Master Control.

2.0.1
Released 15 Oct 2026

Hotfix

Fixes a crash when Zs'Skayr or a Vilgax drone took off from the ground.

2.1.0
In progress

Living Null Void

Smarter prisoners, Null Guardians, Lepidopterran scouts, floating prison blocks with the Prison Warden and a key-locked vault, wrecked Plumber outposts, Taydenite, Plumber blaster, armour and glider, flight animations for Heatblast, Stinkfly and Ghostfreak, balanced armour and tools. From playtesting: camera height that matches the alien's size, a transformation burst and morph for every alien, rapid-fire powers, powers that damage bosses, a walk-through Null Void portal that can banish mobs, bosses that talk in chat, Plumber energy bolts, safer Null Void arrival.
